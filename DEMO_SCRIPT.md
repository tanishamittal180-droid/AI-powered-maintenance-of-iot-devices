# 3-minute demo walkthrough

1. Open **Overview** and explain the fleet cards and sensor telemetry chart.
2. Open **Live Monitor**, choose `Degradation event`, and generate a reading.
3. Explain that the reading is simulated and the alert is a decision-support example.
4. Open **Failure Predictions** and adjust the risk threshold to see how flagged rows change.
5. Open **Analytics** and show the time trends and correlation heatmap.
6. Open **Model Center**, train the model, and explain precision/recall and the confusion matrix.
7. Test a custom reading and show the estimated risk.
8. Close by explaining the main limitation: synthetic labels are only for demonstrating the workflow.
